How it works

01  Each model gets three prompts: analyze this person, what are they known for, and would you shortlist them — with web search on, the way a real person would ask.
02  A separate grader reads the raw answers and scores five things: Recognition (does it know who this is), Depth, Sources (how many independent domains), Recommendation (would it shortlist), and Accuracy when a facts file is supplied.
03  The Footprint Score is the average across models. Bands: Invisible Thin Visible Undeniable. Every raw answer is kept on the page — nothing is paraphrased.
